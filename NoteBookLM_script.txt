======================================================================
HISSA 1: KYA HAI "NAQATAR"?
======================================================================

Naqatar ek online clinic appointment app hai. Is ke zariye log apne
shehar ke clinics mein ghar baithe appointment book kar sakte hain:
line mein lagne ki zaroorat nahi, baar baar phone karne ki zaroorat
nahi, aur intezar ka koi andaza lagane ki zaroorat bhi nahi.

Naqatar do qisam ke clinics ke liye bana hai:

1. Medical clinics: general doctor, dentist, bachon ke doctor,
   physiotherapy, eye clinic, lab tests waghera.
2. Aesthetic clinics: skin aur laser treatment, HydraFacial,
   hair loss treatment, cosmetic procedures waghera.

Naqatar ki zubaan Hinglish hai, yani Roman Urdu aur aam English ke
lafz, bilkul waise jaise hum roz WhatsApp par likhte hain. Is liye
har koi isay asaani se samajh aur chala sakta hai.

Naqatar ek website hai jo mobile app ki tarah bhi chalti hai.
Android ya iPhone par isay "Install" ya "Add to Home Screen" karke
phone ki home screen par rakha ja sakta hai. Phir yeh ek aam app ki
tarah apne icon se khulti hai. Play Store ya App Store se download
karne ki zaroorat nahi.

Naam ke baare mein: Naqatar ka logo ek dhaal (shield) hai, jis ke
andar ek aath-konon wala sitara aur "نقطار" likha hai. Dhaal hifazat
aur bharose ki nishani hai, aur sitara rehnumai ki. Brand ke rang
gehra teal (sabz-neela) aur sunehri (gold) hain.

======================================================================
HISSA 2: KAAM KAISE KARTA HAI?
======================================================================

--- Customer ke liye: 5 aasaan qadam ---

Qadam 1: Clinic dhoondein
Naqatar ki home screen par sirf clinic ka phone number likhein, kisi
bhi tarah: 0300..., 92300..., ya +92 300... Naqatar us number se
registered clinic dhoond kar seedha us ke page par le jata hai.
Agar number yaad nahi, to "Clinics" list mein Medical aur Aesthetic
tabs, aur clinic ke naam ya area se search bhi hai.

Qadam 2: Clinic ka page dekhein
Har clinic ke page par us ki services hoti hain, har service ki
qeemat aur waqt (maslan "General Consultation: 20 min, Rs 1500"),
clinic ki timings (kis din kab khulta hai), aur pata.

Qadam 3: Service, din aur waqt chunein
Customer ek ya zyada services chunta hai, phir calendar se din, aur
phir sirf khaali waqt (slots) mein se apna waqt. Jo waqt pehle se
bhar chuka ho, ya jo guzar chuka ho, woh nazar hi nahi aata. Waqt
Din, Shaam aur Raat ke hisson mein dikhte hain taa ke chunna aasaan
ho.

Qadam 4: Login aur tasdeeq
Booking se pehle ek chhota sa login hota hai, Google se ya email se.
Is ka maqsad yeh hai ke koi jaali ya mazaq wali booking na ho.
Kuch clinics advance payment maangte hain. Aisi soorat mein customer
bank transfer ka screenshot upload karta hai, ya chahe to clinic par
pahunch kar cash dene ka option chun sakta hai.

Qadam 5: Booking mil gayi
Booking hote hi customer ko ek Booking ID milti hai, aur email par
tafseel bhi aa jati hai. Booking pehle "Pending" hoti hai, aur jab
clinic usay confirm karta hai to customer ko notification milti hai.
Naqatar appointment se 24 ghante, 1 ghanta aur 15 minute pehle yaad
dihani bhi karata hai.

"Meri Bookings" mein customer apni aane wali aur guzri hui saari
appointments dekh sakta hai, aur zaroorat ho to wajah bata kar
appointment cancel bhi kar sakta hai.

--- Clinic ke liye: setup aur rozana ka kaam ---

Register karna:
Clinic owner "Apna Clinic Register Karein" se account banata hai,
clinic ka naam, pata, phone number aur qisam (Medical ya Aesthetic)
batata hai. Naqatar team har naye clinic ko check karke approve
karti hai, taa ke platform par sirf asli clinics hon. Is dauran
owner apni profile aur services tayyar kar sakta hai.

Clinic setup:
- Services add karna: naam, qeemat, kitna waqt lagti hai, aur photo.
- Timings: har din ke khulne aur band hone ka waqt.
- Har appointment kitne minute ki hai, aur ek waqt mein kitni
  bookings ho sakti hain (maslan 2 dental chairs ho to 2).
- Logo aur clinic ki tafseel.

Rozana ka kaam (Clinic Dashboard):
- Aaj ki, pending, confirmed aur mukammal appointments ki ginti.
- Har appointment par ek click: Confirm, Cancel, "Nahi aaye"
  (No-Show), ya Mukammal.
- Advance payment wale customers ke screenshot dekh kar confirm karna.
- Appointments ki list ko tareekh se filter karna aur CSV/Excel mein
  download karna.

Double booking ka masla khatam:
Jab do log ek hi waqt par aakhri khaali jagah book karne ki koshish
karein, to Naqatar sirf ek ki booking hone deta hai. Doosre ko foran
bataya jata hai ke yeh waqt abhi abhi le liya gaya, koi aur waqt
chunein. Is tarah clinic ke paas kabhi ek hi waqt par zaroorat se
zyada log nahi aate.

======================================================================
HISSA 3: KYA FAIDA HAI?
======================================================================

--- Customer ke liye faide ---

1. Waqt ki bachat: clinic jaa kar line mein lagne ya ghanton intezar
   karne ki zaroorat nahi. Pehle se pata hota hai ke kab jana hai.
2. 24 ghante booking: clinic band ho tab bhi, raat ko bhi, booking ki
   ja sakti hai. Phone uthne ka intezar nahi.
3. Sab kuch saaf: services ki qeemat aur waqt pehle se nazar aate
   hain, koi andaza nahi lagana padta.
4. Yaad dihani: appointment bhoolne ka darr nahi. Email, notification
   aur reminders khud aate hain.
5. Sirf number se clinic: clinic ka number pata ho to bas likhein,
   seedha clinic ka page khul jata hai.
6. Apni zubaan: Hinglish mein, saada aur aasaan.
7. Ek jagah saari bookings: "Meri Bookings" mein poora record.
8. Customer ke liye Naqatar istemal karna muft hai. Ilaj ki fees
   wahi hai jo clinic leta hai.

--- Clinic / Business ke liye faide ---

1. Kam phone calls: receptionist ka bara waqt booking ke phone sunne
   mein jata hai. Naqatar par customer khud booking karta hai.
2. Munazzam din: har appointment ka waqt, service aur customer ki
   maloomat pehle se maloom. Rush aur khaali waqt dono kam hote hain.
3. Double booking nahi: system khud har slot ki gintee rakhta hai.
4. Jaali bookings mein kami: login zaroori hai, aur chahein to advance
   payment ka option on kar sakte hain.
5. "Nahi aaye" ka hisaab: No-Show mark karne se slot dobara khul jata
   hai aur record bhi rehta hai.
6. Online pehchaan: har clinic ka apna page, services, qeematein,
   timings aur logo ke saath, jo WhatsApp par share bhi ho sakta hai.
7. Naye customers: log Medical aur Aesthetic tabs mein clinics
   dhoondte hain, aur wahan aap ka clinic nazar aata hai.
8. Record aur report: appointments ki list Excel/CSV mein download
   karke hisaab kitaab aasaan.
9. Shuru karna aasaan: koi software install nahi, koi machine nahi.
   Phone ya computer ke browser se poora clinic chalaya ja sakta hai.
   Naye clinics ke liye free trial bhi hai.

KHULASA (Summary)
======================================================================

Naqatar Medical aur Aesthetic clinics ke liye ek aasaan Hinglish
appointment app hai. Customer clinic ka number likhta hai, service,
din aur khaali waqt chunta hai, aur booking ho jati hai. Saath mein
Booking ID, email aur reminders bhi milte hain. Clinic ek dashboard
se apni saari appointments, services aur timings sambhalta hai, aur
double booking ya jaali booking ki fikr nahi karta.

Customer ke liye: kam intezar, saaf qeematein, aur yaad dihani.
Clinic ke liye: kam phone calls, munazzam din, aur naye customers.
